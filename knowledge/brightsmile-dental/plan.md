# BrightSmile Dental plan: Standard

## What the plan includes
The Standard plan costs $99 per month and includes up to **8 change requests per month**.
Included types of change:
- Text and content updates (services, team bios, hours, contact details).
- Image changes with photos supplied by the client.
- Style changes that follow the brand guide.
- New sections on existing pages (for example an FAQ or an insurance section).

## What the plan does not include
- New pages beyond 2 per year.
- Online booking systems or patient portals; these require integration with practice software.
- Online payments.
- Anything that stores patient health information on the website.

## Response times
Requests are handled within 2 business days.
