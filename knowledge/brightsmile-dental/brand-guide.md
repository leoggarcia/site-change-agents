# BrightSmile Dental brand guide

## Personality and tone of voice
Calm, reassuring and clear. Many patients are anxious, so avoid words like "pain", "drill" or
"needle" in headings. Use "you" and "your family". Medical claims must be accurate; never promise
results ("perfect teeth", "guaranteed").

## Colors
- BrightSmile blue `#0b6fa4` (`--brand-blue`): headings and the logo text.
- Mint `#3cc6a8` (`--brand-mint`): call-to-action buttons and accents.
- Light blue surface `#eef7fb` (`--surface-alt`): alternate section backgrounds.
- Ink `#12303f` (`--ink`): body text and footer background.
"The main color" or "our blue" means BrightSmile blue. "The green button" means mint.

## Typography
Helvetica Neue / Arial for everything. Headings are bold, never italic.

## Photography and images
Photos of the real team and the real office only. No stock photos of models smiling.
Patient photos only with written consent on file.

## Rules
- Doctors are always written with their credentials, e.g. "Dr. Emily Chen, DDS".
- Every page must keep the "Book a visit" button in the header.
- The emergency phone number must always be visible on the home page.
