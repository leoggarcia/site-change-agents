# Luna Café brand guide

## Personality and tone of voice
Warm, unhurried and neighborly. Write like a friendly barista talking to a regular: short sentences,
first person plural ("we roast", "we bake"), no exclamation marks in headings. Avoid corporate words
such as "solutions", "premium experience" or "world-class".

## Colors
- Espresso brown `#6b3e26` (`--color-primary`): headings, header background, links.
- Honey `#e9a03b` (`--color-accent`): buttons and highlights only, never for body text.
- Cream `#fbf7f2` (`--color-bg`): page background.
- Dark roast `#2b211b` (`--color-text`): body text and footer background.
When the client says "our brand color" they mean espresso brown; "the orange" or "the yellow" means honey.

## Typography
Headings use Georgia (serif). Body text uses the system sans-serif font. Do not add web fonts;
the owner wants the site to stay fast on old phones.

## Photography and images
Only real photos taken in the café by the owner. Never use stock photos or AI-generated images.
Food photos must show the actual product sold at Luna Café.

## Rules
- Prices always include the dollar sign and two decimals, e.g. "$4.50".
- Opening hours are written as "7:00 am – 7:00 pm" with an en dash.
- The café name is always written "Luna Café" with the accent.
