# Luna Café plan: Basic

## What the plan includes
The Basic plan costs $49 per month and includes up to **3 change requests per month**.
Included types of change:
- Text and content updates (prices, menu items, opening hours, phone, address).
- Replacing images with photos supplied by the client.
- Small style tweaks to existing sections (colors, spacing, font sizes) that follow the brand guide.

## What the plan does not include
- New pages or new sections. These need the Standard plan or a one-off quote.
- Online ordering, online payments, an online store or any e-commerce feature.
- Booking or reservation systems.
- Integrations with third-party services (newsletters, delivery apps, social media feeds).
- Copywriting from scratch; the client provides the text.

## Response times
Requests are handled within 2 business days. Urgent changes (wrong price or wrong opening hours)
are handled the same business day.

## Upgrading
Clients can move to the Standard plan ($99 per month) at any time to get new sections and up to
8 requests per month.
