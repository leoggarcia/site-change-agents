# Northside Accounting brand guide

## Personality and tone of voice
Professional, precise and plain-spoken. No jargon without explanation, no hype. Numbers are
always exact. Write in the first person plural ("we prepare", "we file").

## Colors
- Navy `#1d2b45` (`--navy`): header, footer and headings.
- Gold `#c8a24a` (`--gold`): thin accents, the logo text and the highlighted pricing plan border.
- Paper `#f6f5f1` (`--paper`): page background.
"Our brand color" means navy. Gold is used sparingly and never for large backgrounds.

## Typography
Headings use Palatino (serif). Body text uses Segoe UI / Roboto.

## Rules
- Prices are written as "$149 / month".
- Certifications are listed exactly as issued: "Certified Public Accountants (CPA)".
- Do not publish client names or testimonials without written permission.
