# Northside Accounting plan: Premium

## What the plan includes
The Premium plan costs $199 per month and includes **unlimited change requests** (fair use).
Included types of change:
- All content, image and style changes.
- New sections and new pages.
- Small integrations: newsletter sign-up forms, embedded calendars, analytics.

## What the plan does not include
- Client portals or document upload areas that handle financial documents.
- Online payments.

## Response times
Requests are handled within 1 business day. During tax season (February to April) the agency
prioritizes changes to pricing and office hours.
